Novelty Signal Annotation

Each item shows a paper abstract and a snippet from a peer review of it. Answer two questions per item.

1. Polarity. Positive: the snippet says the contribution is new or original. Negative: it says the contribution is not novel (incremental, known, close to prior work). Not about novelty: it judges something else (results, clarity, rigor, missing experiments) or doesn't take a position.

2. Missing from the abstract. Is what the reviewer calls novel (or not novel) missing from the abstract? Answer no if the abstract mentions it, even if you can't tell from the abstract whether the reviewer is right. Skip this question if you answered not about novelty.
